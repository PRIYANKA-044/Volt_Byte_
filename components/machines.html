Electrical Machines

DC Motor

Function

Converts electrical energy into mechanical energy using direct current supply.

Applications

  • Electric Vehicles
  • Conveyor Systems
  • Robotics
  • Industrial Drives

DC Generator

Function

Converts mechanical energy into direct current electrical energy.

Applications

  • Battery Charging
  • Laboratory Experiments
  • Backup Power Systems
  • Training Purposes

Transformer

Function

Transfers electrical energy between circuits and changes voltage levels.

Applications

  • Power Transmission
  • Distribution Systems
  • Substations
  • Electronics

Induction Motor

Function

Operates on electromagnetic induction and is widely used in industries.

Applications

  • Pumps
  • Fans
  • Compressors
  • Industrial Machinery

Synchronous Motor

Function

Runs at constant synchronous speed regardless of load variations.

Applications

  • Power Factor Correction
  • Industrial Drives
  • Large Compressors
  • Power Plants

Alternator

Function

Converts mechanical energy into alternating current electrical energy.

Applications

  • Power Stations
  • Automobiles
  • Diesel Generators
  • Renewable Energy Systems
