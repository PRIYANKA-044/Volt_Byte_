Basic Electrical & Electronic Components

Resistor

Definition

A resistor is a passive electronic component that opposes the flow of electric current and controls voltage and current levels in circuits.

Functions

  • Current Limiting
  • Voltage Division
  • Circuit Protection
  • Biasing Electronic Devices

Applications

  • LED Circuits
  • Amplifiers
  • Power Supplies
  • Electronic Devices

Advantages

  • Simple Construction
  • Low Cost
  • Reliable Operation
  • Widely Used in Electronics

Capacitor

Definition

A capacitor stores electrical energy in an electric field and releases it when required by the circuit.

Functions

  • Energy Storage
  • Filtering
  • Coupling and Decoupling
  • Noise Reduction

Applications

  • SMPS
  • Audio Circuits
  • Motor Starters
  • Power Electronics

Advantages

  • Fast Charging and Discharging
  • Compact Size
  • Long Life
  • High Efficiency

Inductor

Definition

An inductor stores energy in a magnetic field when current flows through it.

Functions

  • Energy Storage
  • Filtering
  • Noise Reduction
  • Signal Processing

Applications

  • Power Supplies
  • DC Converters
  • Communication Systems
  • RF Circuits

Advantages

  • Efficient Energy Storage
  • Noise Filtering
  • Reliable Operation
  • Long Service Life

Transformer

Definition

A transformer transfers electrical energy between circuits through electromagnetic induction.

Functions

  • Step-Up Voltage
  • Step-Down Voltage
  • Electrical Isolation
  • Power Transfer

Applications

  • Power Transmission
  • Chargers
  • UPS Systems
  • Substations

Advantages

  • High Efficiency
  • Reliable Operation
  • Electrical Isolation
  • Low Maintenance

Fuse

Definition

A fuse protects electrical circuits by melting when excessive current flows through it.

Functions

  • Overload Protection
  • Short Circuit Protection
  • Equipment Safety

Applications

  • Home Wiring
  • Industrial Systems
  • Electronic Equipment
  • Power Distribution

Advantages

  • Low Cost
  • Easy Replacement
  • Reliable Protection
  • Simple Design

LED (Light Emitting Diode)

Definition

LED is a semiconductor device that emits light when electric current passes through it.

Functions

  • Produces Light
  • Status Indication
  • Visual Signalling
  • Display Applications

Applications

  • Indicator Lamps
  • Display Panels
  • Traffic Signals
  • Street Lighting

Advantages

  • Low Power Consumption
  • Long Life Span
  • High Efficiency
  • Fast Switching

Switch

Definition

A switch is a device used to open or close an electrical circuit.

Functions

  • Control Current Flow
  • Turn Devices ON/OFF
  • Safety Control

Applications

  • Home Appliances
  • Control Panels
  • Industrial Systems
  • Electronic Devices

Advantages

  • Easy Operation
  • Reliable Control
  • Low Cost
  • Long Service Life

Battery

Definition

A battery converts chemical energy into electrical energy and supplies power to devices.

Functions

  • Energy Storage
  • Portable Power Source
  • Backup Power

Applications

  • Mobile Phones
  • Electric Vehicles
  • UPS Systems
  • Solar Energy Systems

Advantages

  • Portable
  • Rechargeable Options Available
  • Reliable Power Source
  • Easy Installation

Potentiometer

Definition

A potentiometer is a variable resistor used to adjust voltage, current and signal levels in electronic circuits.

Functions

  • Variable Resistance Control
  • Voltage Adjustment
  • Signal Control
  • Circuit Calibration

Applications

  • Volume Control
  • Motor Speed Control
  • Light Dimmer Circuits
  • Industrial Panels

Advantages

  • Easy to Adjust
  • Low Cost
  • Accurate Control
  • Widely Used
