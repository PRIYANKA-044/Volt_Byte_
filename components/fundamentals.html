Electrical Fundamentals

Voltage

Definition

Voltage is the electrical potential difference between two points.

Unit

Volt (V)

Formula

V = I × R

Where Used

  • Power Transmission
  • Electronic Circuits
  • Electrical Machines
  • Household Wiring

Applications

  • Mobile Chargers
  • Industrial Systems
  • Electric Vehicles

Current

Definition

Current is the flow of electric charge.

Unit

Ampere (A)

Formula

I = V / R

Where Used

  • Motors
  • Pumps
  • Fans
  • Industrial Loads

Applications

  • Motor Drives
  • Battery Systems
  • Electronic Devices

Resistance

Definition

Opposition offered to current flow.

Unit

Ohm (Ω)

Formula

R = V / I

Where Used

  • LED Circuits
  • Voltage Dividers
  • Current Limiting

Applications

  • Circuit Protection
  • Signal Conditioning

Electrical Power

Definition

Rate of electrical energy transfer.

Unit

Watt (W)

Formula

P = V × I

Applications

  • Motors
  • Generators
  • Industries
  • Home Appliances

Electrical Energy

Definition

Capacity to perform work.

Unit

Joule (J) / kWh

Formula

Energy = Power × Time

Applications

  • Energy Billing
  • Solar Plants
  • Battery Systems

AC Supply

Definition

Current changes direction periodically.

Frequency

50 Hz (India)

Applications

  • Homes
  • Industries
  • Power Distribution

DC Supply

Definition

Current flows in one direction only.

Sources

Battery, Solar Panel, DC Generator

Applications

  • Mobile Phones
  • Laptops
  • Embedded Systems
  • Electric Vehicles

Ohm's Law

Definition

Relationship between Voltage, Current and Resistance.

Formula

V = I × R

Applications

  • Circuit Analysis
  • Load Calculation
  • Electrical Design

Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)

Definition

Total current entering a node equals total current leaving the node.

Applications

  • Circuit Analysis
  • Network Design

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

Definition

Sum of voltages around a closed loop is zero.

Applications

  • Electrical Networks
  • Power Systems

Power Factor

Definition

Ratio of real power to apparent power.

Formula

PF = Cos φ

Applications

  • Industries
  • Power System Efficiency

Three Phase Supply

Definition

System containing three AC voltages 120° apart.

Applications

  • Industries
  • Large Motors
  • Power Distribution
